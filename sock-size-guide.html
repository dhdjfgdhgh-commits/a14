Sock Size Guide

A sock that fits sits with its heel on your heel, its toe seam past your toes and its cuff resting comfortably. Here is how to get there.

1

Measure your foot

Stand on a sheet of paper, mark the back of your heel and the tip of your longest toe, and measure the distance. Do it late in the day, when feet are at their largest, and measure both feet: use the longer one.

2

Read the size chart

Typical ranges. Brands vary, so check their own chart.

3

Between sizes?

Go up for thick, cushioned or wool socks, and for wide feet. Go down for thin dress socks and anything with lots of elastane, which will stretch to fit.

4

Check the heel

The shaped heel pocket should cup your heel. If it sits up on the back of your ankle, the sock is too small; if it slides under your foot, it is too big. Both lead to rubbing and blisters.

5

Check the cuff

A cuff that leaves a deep mark is too tight. Look for “comfort” or loose-top cuffs if you have wide calves or swelling, and wider ribbed tops for knee-highs.
