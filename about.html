about footbed

Small thing, big difference.

Footbed is an independent website all about socks.

Socks are easy to ignore until they slip, rub, wear through or leave your feet cold and damp. We explain sizes, fibres, cushioning and care in a friendly, plain way, with small tools that help you choose.

We do not sell socks, take payment from brands or use affiliate links. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
